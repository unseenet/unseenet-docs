# Names

A key is hard to say out loud. A **name** like `@lorensen` is a label on
your key that others can type and check. You do not need one: you can
read, write and be written to without.

A name links everything its identity posts. Do not claim one for an
identity you keep apart.

## The kinds of name

- **A name without a dot**, like `lorensen`, is first come, first served.
  Names that are Internet top-level domains (`dk`, `microsoft`) cannot be
  had.
- **A domain you own**, like `lorensen.dk`: the client tells you a DNS
  record to add first, and the name is renewed each month while the
  record is there.
- **A name under someone's name**, like `sonia@lorensen`: its holder gives
  it, with their own key - like e-mail addresses in a family or a club.
  No registrar is involved.

A claim is sent sealed to the registrar, which decides after about an
hour; the answer comes to your private posts. Registered names last a
year and are renewed from the same place.

## Claiming a name

=== "Graphical"

    **Names** (`@`, or ++ctrl+p++): type the name under *Your names* and
    **Claim**. *A name under someone else's* asks its holder for one.

    ![Names: claim yours, or ask for a name under someone else's](../img/guide/names.png)

=== "Terminal"

    `@`, type the name and ++enter++. **Give a name under yours** and
    **Ask for a name under someone's name** are in the palette.

    ![Your names (@): the names you hold, and a field to claim a new one](../img/client-names.svg)

## Petnames win

Whatever names others hold, the name **you** gave a contact always wins in
your client. That is what keeps a look-alike name from fooling you.

## On a website or in a signature

Outside Unseenet, write it as `Unseenet: andreas@lorensen` and link it to
your key - [Show your name elsewhere](show-your-name.md) has the icon and
snippets to copy.
